How it works

The bot checks the live price every few seconds. If the price is at or below your buy threshold it buys; at or above your sell threshold it sells.

The buy threshold must be below the sell threshold, otherwise both rules would fire at once.

When you fast-forward time, the bot is evaluated against every bar it skips through, so it makes the trades it would have made during that period.
