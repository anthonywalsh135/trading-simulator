Privacy and your data

How this simulator handles your personal information, under the UK GDPR.

What we store

DataWhy
First nameShown on your account, the leaderboard and to friends.
Email addressUsed to log in and to send one-time verification codes.
PasswordStored only as a scrypt hash, never as text, and not recoverable.
Trading activityYour simulated balance, holdings and trade history.
Friend connectionsWho you have connected with, so portfolios can be compared.

All trading is simulated. No real money, bank details or payment information is ever collected, and nothing is shared with third parties.

Your rights

  • Access. Your account page shows every piece of data held about you.
  • Rectification. You can change your name at any time from your account page.
  • Erasure. Deleting your account permanently removes your details, holdings, trade history and friend connections.

Third-party data

Market prices come from public Yahoo Finance and Binance endpoints. Only the asset symbol you are viewing is sent to them, never your identity or any account data.
